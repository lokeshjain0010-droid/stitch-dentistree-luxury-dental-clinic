## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier clinical sanctuary tailored for elite oral healthcare and implantology. Built for an audience seeking supreme surgical reliability, cosmetic mastery, and hospitality-grade comfort, the interface rejects cold hospital sterility in favor of tranquil, luxurious precision.

The style fuses **Corporate Modern** rigor with **Tactile Minimalist** refinement:
- High-clarity information architecture designed to alleviate patient anxiety and facilitate immediate appointment booking.
- Controlled white space evoking surgical hygiene, counterbalanced by deep structural navy and soothing teal treatments.
- Understated architectural lines and micro-interactions that inspire authoritative trust and effortless conversion.

## Colors

The color system combines serene restorative teal with authoritative navy foundations and warm brushed-gold undertones:

- **Primary (`#0D9488`)**: The signature healing teal, utilized for primary calls-to-action, active booking steps, and health verification indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0B192C`)**: Deep midnight navy providing high-contrast readability, grounding structural headers, footers, and brand iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted champagne gold dedicated to premium tiers, cosmetic dentistry badges, and subtle decorative accents.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Balanced slate gray managing secondary copy, outlines, and structural layout dividers without creating harsh visual breaks.
- **Surface Foundations**: Crisp porcelain white (`#FFFFFF`) with warm surgical off-white (`#F8FAFC`) background tiers to eliminate glare while maintaining pure clinical clarity.

## Typography

Typography pairs geometric approachability with rigorous technical legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric warmth with friendly terminals and confident structures. Generous negative space between letterforms keeps high-density titles approachable and refined.
- **Body & Editorial (`Inter`)**: Engineered for absolute clinical clarity, high legibility across treatment descriptions, diagnostic reports, and medical forms.
- **Eyebrow Labels**: Uppercase styling with expanded letter spacing (`0.04em`–`0.05em`) in semi-bold Plus Jakarta Sans establishes quiet luxury across service tags and doctor specializations.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced, rhythmic 12-column responsive grid system prioritizes composure, breathing room, and structured conversion flows:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout framed by 3rem (`margin`) canvas gutters and 1.5rem (`gutter`) column channels. Content max-width is anchored at 1280px for standard flows and 1440px for full-width clinical showcases.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.25rem gutters. Hero split sections collapse gracefully to vertical step-down stacks.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout with 1.25rem outer safe margins. Floating CTA panels lock to bottom navigation bars to keep consultation booking within thumb reach.
- **Rhythm**: Internal card padding uses generous multi-tier spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to prevent cramped data density and communicate serene exclusivity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering coupled with soft, tinted ambient shadows rather than rigid physical borders:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Ultra-clean `#F8FAFC` foundation providing a calm, non-glare surface.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Containers)**: Solid pure white `#FFFFFF` surface lifted by an ambient tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Booking Elements)**: Lifted state elevating the component with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08)`, introducing a gentle primary teal aura.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Navbars)**: High-order overlays using frosted glassmorphism: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12)`.
- **Dividers & Hairlines**: Minimal 1px borders tinted to `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.12)` maintain spatial order without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a disciplined **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape scale:

- **Core Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to interactive form fields, checkboxes, list items, and utility tags, providing structured balance.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / `16px`)**: Utilized across diagnostic cards, doctor bio dossiers, service highlights, and modal dialogues.
- **Feature Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Reserved for primary hero visual cards, testimonial enclosures, and booking engine summary modules.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status badges (e.g., "NABH Accredited", "Immediate Slot Available") and high-priority primary CTAs to maximize tap accessibility.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Consultation)**: Background in teal (`#0D9488`), text in white (`#FFFFFF`), full pill or 8px radius, medium weight typography. Hover state incorporates a subtle deep-teal transition (`#0F766E`) with an elevated teal shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Explore Treatments)**: Navy boundary outline (`1.5px solid #0B192C`), navy text, transparent background. Hover state fills to midnight navy (`#0B192C`) with white text.
- **Tertiary / Concierge Action**: Champagne gold accents (`#C5A880`) for VIP, international patient, and implant consultation direct lines.

### Cards
- **Treatment Cards**: Pristine white surfaces with 16px corner radii. Contains service micro-iconography, headline title in navy, brief procedure description in slate neutral, and an inline link with a smooth forward arrow transition.
- **Doctor Dossier Cards**: Two-part layout featuring crisp medical photography with soft tinted backgrounds, highlighted surgical designations, qualification tags, and immediate calendar selection buttons.

### Inputs & Booking Forms
- **Field Styling**: 48px height, 8px border radius, subtle slate border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), warm white background. Focus state transitions to a crisp teal border (`#0D9488`) paired with a 3px soft focus ring (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`).
- **Labels**: Persistent top-aligned labels rendered in `label-md` uppercase typography for effortless reading.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Specialty Tags**: Soft teal wash (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08)`) with sharp teal text (`#0D9488`), pill-shaped with compact padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`).
- **Premium Indicators**: Muted champagne tinted pill (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.15)`) with `#8C6B3E` typography.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Controls**: Custom 20px rounded squares (checkboxes) and circles (radios) with 1.5px slate borders. Checked states shift to solid `#0D9488` with white iconography.

### Specialized Clinical Modules
- **Before / After Comparison Slider**: Clean 16px rounded framed container with an interactive pill divider handle in teal and navy, allowing frictionless inspection of smile transformations.
- **Doctor Availability Matrix**: Time-slot chips organized horizontally with active/inactive states clearly distinguished by subtle color fills and primary teal selection rings.
